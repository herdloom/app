# Code of conduct

This is a small project with a small community. The rules are short.

**Be decent.** Assume the other person is trying to get work done, same as you.
Criticise the software, not the person.

**Keep it useful.** Bug reports with steps, ideas with the problem behind them,
answers that help the next reader. No spam, no recruiting, no self-promotion.

**Respect privacy.** Do not post other people's code, tokens or customer data.
Redact before you paste.

**No harassment**, of any kind, in issues, discussions or reviews. That includes
personal attacks, demeaning comments, and unwelcome attention.

Reports go to the repository owner through a
[private advisory](../../security/advisories/new) or a direct message. Anything
reported is handled privately. Breaking these rules gets your comment removed;
doing it again gets you blocked.
